CardDock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Mochido Studio ("we," "us," or "our") sets out below how information about users is handled in "CardDock" (the "App"), an iPhone app.

This English version is provided for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information We Collect

We do not operate any server that collects users' personal information. The App does not require account registration and does not send your name, email address, location, or similar information to us. The App does not show ads.

2. Information Handled on Your iPhone

The following information is stored only on your iPhone (in an area shared by the App and its widgets) so that the App's features can work:

  • Settings such as the list of apps you have added to the App, their order, groups, and theme colors
  • Images imported as image cards (chosen from your photos). Location data and other metadata are removed when they are imported. The original photos in the Photos app are not changed
  • A cache of the icon images of the apps you have added

This information is removed from your iPhone when you delete the App.

3. iCloud

  • If iCloud is enabled on your iPhone, the App's data (the settings and images described in Section 2) is backed up to your own iCloud and can be restored when you change devices. We cannot access this data.
  • We may also store very small setting values, such as the date you received the early download bonus, in your own iCloud.
  • You can stop saving to iCloud by turning off the App in the iPhone Settings app > Apple ID > iCloud.

4. Communication with External Services

The App connects to the internet only for the following purposes. In none of these cases do we send information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.

  • Apple's App Store (iTunes Search API): To search for apps and to fetch official icons. Your search terms and app IDs are sent to Apple.
  • Apple's App Store (StoreKit): To purchase and restore the Pro plan. Purchase information is managed by Apple, and we do not receive credit card numbers or similar information.
  • Apple's iCloud (Shortcuts): When you open the link for adding the launch shortcut.
  • Our official website (mochidostudio.com): When the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy are displayed in the App. The server that delivers them may keep access logs. If the App cannot connect, it shows the same content bundled with the App.

5. Interaction Between Apps

The App opens the apps you have added using iOS mechanisms (URL schemes, universal links, and the Shortcuts app). When this happens, the provider of the app being opened may collect information on its own; such collection is governed by that company's privacy policy.

6. Analytics and Tracking

The App does not use usage analytics tools or advertising tracking. It also does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission.

7. Use by Children

The App is not directed at any particular age group and does not collect information about age.

8. Changes

We may change this Policy as necessary. If we do, we will post it in the App and update the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For inquiries about this Policy, please contact support@mochidostudio.com.
